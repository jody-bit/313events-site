Accessibility Statement

313.events aims to be usable by as many people as possible, including people using assistive technology. This is an ongoing effort, not a claim of full or certified compliance.

What we've built in

  • Real, semantic interactive elements (buttons and links) throughout the site, rather than non-interactive elements made to look clickable.
  • Descriptive alt text on meaningful images, with decorative images marked so screen readers skip them.
  • Visible keyboard focus indicators on interactive elements.
  • Reduced-motion handling for visitors whose system is set to minimize animation.
  • Labeled form fields on the event-submission form.

Known limitations

313.events is a small, actively-developed product. We haven't completed a full, formal accessibility audit against a specific standard (such as WCAG 2.1 AA), and some areas of the site may not yet meet every best practice. We're working on this incrementally rather than all at once.

Reporting an issue

If you run into an accessibility barrier using 313.events, please let us know — what page you were on, what you were trying to do, and what happened. We'll look into it.
