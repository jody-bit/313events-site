For venues, promoters, and organizers to add their own listing to the 313.events Arts & Events Calendar — covering Detroit and everywhere within 75 miles — the same self-service model Cleveland's calendar uses (organizations register, log in, and submit their own events).

Coming soon: account sign-in for registered venues/organizers, so submissions are tied to a verified account. For now, every submission is reviewed by hand before it goes live.
What this is: a real submission form backed by a database. Your event is saved to a moderation queue and reviewed by hand before it appears on the calendar — this helps prevent spam and lets us check for duplicates against the other sources already feeding the calendar.
