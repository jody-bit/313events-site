## How to approve one record

Each record is its own decision. To approve, the approving person adds `"approval": { "by": "<name>", "at": "YYYY-MM-DD" }` to that record in `entries.json`, sets `status` to `PUBLISHED`, and runs `node scripts/build-seasonal.js`. The tests refuse a `PUBLISHED` status that the gate does not derive. Nothing in this repository writes an approval. To decline or hold a record, leave it as is. Swapping an image later changes only the record's `image` block, not its verified facts.

Preview: open `/fall?qa=1&launch=1` and `/fall-color?qa=1&launch=1` on a Vercel Preview of this branch. The **Launch preview** button in the QA bar switches between the full QA review and the proposed launch records drawn with the public card layout (hero, filters and cards unchanged). It exists only on Previews and local development.
