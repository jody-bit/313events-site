## Long bio (assembled only from About, Editorial Policy, Data Sources and Accessibility; needs Jody's read before publication)
313.events is an independent Detroit-area event discovery and editorial platform. It covers Detroit and the surrounding region through what the site calls the Detroit Orbit: the full area within range of the city, rather than Detroit proper alone.

The product combines automated event aggregation with original editorial curation. Most of what appears on the calendar, the map and individual event pages is aggregated from organizers, venues and public event sources, or submitted directly by organizers and the public. Including an event is not an endorsement or an editorial judgment about it; it means the event was found and fits the site's coverage area. Organizers and the public can submit an event, and organizers or venues can request a correction to a listing.

On the Radar is the editorial component: a curated collection of local press coverage of Detroit-area events, drawn from outlets the site tracks, including Metro Times, WDET and BridgeDetroit. Choosing which coverage to surface and linking it to the right event is an editorial decision, separate from the automated aggregation elsewhere on the site.

313.events does not currently run paid placements, sponsored listings or paid editorial coverage. If that changes, paid material will be labeled near the content itself.

The site aims to be usable by as many people as possible, including people using assistive technology, and publishes an accessibility statement that describes what is built in and what is still limited. It makes no claim of full or certified compliance.

313.events is a product of Sentient Productions LLC, based in Detroit, Michigan.

Always confirm dates, times and venues before making plans.
