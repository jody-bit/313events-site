Sources pulled for this prototype: dia.org, mocadetroit.org, easternmarket.org, livenation.com (Fox Theatre), 313presents.com, detroitoperahouse.org, detroithistorical.org, detroitjazzfest.org, cliffbells.com, 19hz.info, paxahau.com, Dice (El Club listings), and Resident Advisor — pulled via RA's per-week Detroit listing pages (ra.co/events/us/detroit/week/…) for every week from Aug 19 through Oct 31, 2026, not just RA's "Popular" homepage picks (which show only ~10 of RA's 160+ regional listings). Always confirm dates/times/venues with the source before making plans, especially "Venue TBA" listings, which many underground/warehouse parties intentionally keep private until day-of.

Scope note on Hamtramck & Highland Park: both are legally independent cities, but geographically they're enclaves completely surrounded by the City of Detroit, so events there (Paris Bar, Small's, Vault313, etc.) are included in this calendar.

Excluded as genuinely outside metro Detroit (RA and 19hz both tag broader "Detroit region" listings, but these venues sit in separate suburbs, not enclaves): Necto (Ann Arbor), 215 West (Ferndale), Pine Knob, Meadow Brook, and Michigan Lottery Amphitheatre (Sterling Heights/Clarkston/Rochester). One listing, "The House of Tarot" at "MAD Arts," was dropped because its venue location couldn't be confirmed at all.

Not included: Facebook Events and Partiful have no public, searchable listing pages to pull from automatically (Facebook requires an authenticated app + review process; Partiful events are private invite links by design). Eventbrite and Edmtrain's event search still render via JavaScript in ways this prototype's fetch tool couldn't reliably scrape past their navigation shell — a production build would use their official APIs/RSS feeds instead of page-scraping. thedetroitilove.com blocks automated fetching via robots.txt and was skipped out of respect for that.
