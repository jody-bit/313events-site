Add 313.events to Your Home Screen

Get one-tap access to the calendar, right from your phone's home screen — no app store needed. It opens full-screen, just like any other app.

iPhone & iPad

Works in Safari. Add to Home Screen isn't available from Chrome or other browsers on iOS — that's an Apple restriction, not a 313.events limitation.

  1. Open 313.events in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share icon in the toolbar (on iPhone, it's along the bottom; on iPad, the top).
  3. Scroll down the share sheet and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add in the top right corner.

The chartreuse dot icon will now sit on your home screen like any other app.

Android

Works in Chrome, which is the default browser on most Android phones.

  1. Open 313.events in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top right corner.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (some phones show Install app instead — either works the same way).
  4. Confirm by tapping Add / Install.

Chrome sometimes shows an Install banner on its own at the bottom of the screen — tap that instead if you see it, it does the same thing.
