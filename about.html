About 313.events

313.events is an independent Detroit-area event discovery and editorial platform.

It covers Detroit and the surrounding region through what the site calls the Detroit Orbit — the full area within range of the city, rather than Detroit proper alone.

The product combines automated event aggregation and discovery with original editorial curation. On the Radar is that editorial component — a curated collection of local press coverage of Detroit-area events. See the Editorial Policy for more on how that works.

313.events is a product of Sentient Productions LLC, based in Detroit, Michigan.

Always confirm dates, times, and venues before making plans.
